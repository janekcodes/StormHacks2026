# D1 · ARPANET

## Hook

The first packet-switched network, with a first message that crashed.

## Caption

ARPANET went live in 1969, connecting four university sites by packet switching. Its first message, LOGIN from UCLA to Stanford, crashed after two letters, but the network grew into the internet.

## Caveat

The label 'first' refers to the first ARPANET message, sent on October 29, 1969.

## Stats

- **First message:** October 29, 1969 (wiki-arpanet)
- **Sites connected:** 4 at first (wiki-arpanet)
- **Technique:** packet switching (wiki-arpanet)
