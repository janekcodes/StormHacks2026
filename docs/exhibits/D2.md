# D2 · Email

## Hook

One message, one symbol: the @ that joined a person to a machine.

## Caption

In 1971 Ray Tomlinson sent the first message between computers over the ARPANET and chose the @ sign to join a user to a machine. His choice of separator became universal.

## Stats

- **First network email:** 1971 (Wikipedia: History of email)
- **Symbol chosen:** the @ sign (Wikipedia: History of email)
- **Sent by:** Ray Tomlinson (Wikipedia: History of email)

## Sources

- Wikipedia: History of email: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_email


## Caveat

The 'first' claim refers to networked email between machines; earlier single-system mailbox programs existed.
