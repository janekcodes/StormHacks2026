# S1 · Y2K

## Hook

Two missing digits, and a worldwide scramble to add them back.

## Caption

As the year 2000 approached, programs that stored years with two digits threatened to read 2000 as 1900. A worldwide remediation effort, estimated in the hundreds of billions of dollars, fixed most problems before they happened.

## Stats

- **Root cause:** two-digit years (Wikipedia: Year 2000 problem)
- **Remediation cost:** hundreds of billions of dollars (Wikipedia: Year 2000 problem)
- **Outcome:** mostly avoided (Wikipedia: Year 2000 problem)
