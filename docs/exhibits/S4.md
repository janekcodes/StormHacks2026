# S4 · Energy & e-waste

## Hook

The cloud has a carbon bill, and the phone in your pocket has a landfill date.

## Caption

Data centres, devices and the race to build new chips all consume energy and create electronic waste. As computing grows, so does the question of how to power and dispose of it.

## Stats

- **Data centres:** about 1 to 1.5 percent of global electricity (IEA: Data centres and data transmission networks)
- **E-waste in 2022:** 62 million tonnes (UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024)
- **Formally collected:** about 22 percent (UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024)

## Sources

- IEA: Data centres and data transmission networks: https://www.iea.org/energy-system/buildings/data-centres-and-data-transmission-networks
- UN Global E-waste Monitor 2024: https://ewastemonitor.info/
