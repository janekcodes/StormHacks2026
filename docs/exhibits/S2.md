# S2 · GDPR & privacy

## Hook

A law that put people, not companies, in charge of their data.

## Caption

The EU's General Data Protection Regulation took effect in May 2018, giving people rights over their personal data. It became a global benchmark for privacy law and can levy fines of up to four percent of global turnover.

## Stats

- **In effect:** May 2018 (Wikipedia: General Data Protection Regulation)
- **Rights:** over personal data (Wikipedia: General Data Protection Regulation)
- **Max fine:** 4 percent of global turnover (Wikipedia: General Data Protection Regulation)
