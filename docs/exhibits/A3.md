# A3 · Bletchley Park: Bombe & Colossus

## Hook

Codebreaking machines that helped shorten a war and pioneered the programmable computer.

## Caption

At Bletchley Park, the Bombe helped break German Enigma messages, and in 1944 Colossus attacked the Lorenz cipher. Colossus used valves and paper tape and is counted as the first programmable electronic digital computer.

## Caveat

The label 'first' applies to Colossus as a programmable electronic digital computer.

## Stats

- **Bombe:** Enigma codebreaking, 1940 (wiki-bombe)
- **Colossus:** Lorenz cipher, 1944 (wiki-colossus)
- **Programmed by:** plugs and switches (wiki-colossus)
