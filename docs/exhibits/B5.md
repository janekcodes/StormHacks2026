# B5 · The integrated circuit

## Hook

A whole circuit, shrunk onto a single piece of silicon.

## Caption

Jack Kilby built the first integrated circuit in 1958, and Robert Noyce made a silicon version in 1959 that was easier to mass produce. Putting many components on one chip made the microelectronics revolution possible.

## Caveat

The label 'first' refers to Kilby's 1958 demonstration and Noyce's 1959 silicon version.

## Stats

- **Kilby's IC:** 1958 (chm-silicon)
- **Noyce's silicon IC:** 1959 (chm-silicon)
- **Nobel Prize:** 2000, to Kilby (wiki-ic)
