# E1 · Spacewar!

## Hook

Two ships, one star, and a game written on a minicomputer in 1962.

## Caption

In 1962 Steve Russell and friends at MIT built Spacewar! on a PDP-1 minicomputer. The two-player space duel is one of the earliest video games and the direct ancestor of the arcade.

## Stats

- **Built:** 1962 (Wikipedia: Spacewar!)
- **Machine:** PDP-1 (Wikipedia: Spacewar!)
- **Place:** MIT (Wikipedia: Spacewar!)

## Caveat
