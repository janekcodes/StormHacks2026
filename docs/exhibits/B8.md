# B8 · Cray-1 supercomputer

## Hook

A machine shaped like a C, built to keep its wires as short as possible.

## Caption

Seymour Cray's Cray-1, shipped in 1976, was shaped like a C to shorten its wiring and defined the supercomputer race of the 1970s and 1980s. It made vector computing famous.

## Stats

- **Shipped:** 1976 (Wikipedia: Cray-1)
- **Shape:** a C, for shorter wires (Wikipedia: Cray-1)
- **Speed:** about 160 megaflops (Wikipedia: Cray-1)

## Sources

- Wikipedia: Cray-1: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cray-1


## Caveat

Performance claims reflect the Cray-1 at its 1976 introduction, relative to machines of its time.
