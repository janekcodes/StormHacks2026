# F5 · AI winters & expert systems

## Hook

Twice the money dried up when the promises ran ahead of the machines.

## Caption

Funding for artificial intelligence collapsed twice, in the mid 1970s and again around 1990, after grand promises failed to arrive. These AI winters followed hype cycles that the field still learns from.

## Stats

- **First winter:** mid 1970s (Wikipedia: AI winter)
- **Second winter:** late 1980s to 1990s (Wikipedia: AI winter)
- **Trigger:** unmet expectations (Wikipedia: AI winter)
