# A2 · Shannon: circuits as Boolean logic

## Hook

One thesis joined algebra to electricity, and every logic gate descends from it.

## Caption

Claude Shannon's 1937 master's thesis showed that electrical switches could carry out Boolean logic. It linked algebra and circuits and is often called the most important master's thesis of the century.

## Stats

- **Thesis:** A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits, 1937 (Wikipedia: A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits)
- **Linked:** Boolean algebra and circuits (Wikipedia: A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits)
