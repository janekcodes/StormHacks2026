# P3 · Jacquard's punched-card loom

## Hook

Punched cards ran a loom two centuries before they ran a computer.

## Caption

Joseph Marie Jacquard's loom wove patterns from a chain of punched cards, so one machine could produce many designs without being rebuilt. The idea of a punched-card program later inspired Babbage and Hollerith.

## Stats

- **Loom:** Jacquard loom, 1804 (Wikipedia: Jacquard machine)
- **Controlled by:** punched cards (Wikipedia: Jacquard machine)
