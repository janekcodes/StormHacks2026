# P4 · Babbage's Difference & Analytical Engines

## Hook

Two engines, one idea: a computer that runs on numbers and never tires.

## Caption

Charles Babbage designed the Difference Engine to compute mathematical tables, and the Analytical Engine to run any calculation from punched cards. Neither was finished in his lifetime, but the Analytical Engine's design held every part of a modern computer: a store, a mill, and control by a program.

## Stats

- **Difference Engine:** conceived 1822 (chm-babbage)
- **Analytical Engine:** designed 1837 (chm-babbage)
- **Design:** store, mill, punched cards (sci-babbage)
