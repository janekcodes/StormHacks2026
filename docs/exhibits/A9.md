# A9 · Post-quantum crypto standards

## Hook

New math, new standards, written before the quantum computer that would break the old ones.

## Caption

In 2024 NIST published the first standardized post-quantum algorithms, designed to resist attack by a quantum computer. They replace today’s key exchange and signatures before a large quantum machine arrives.

## Stats

- **Standards:** FIPS 203, 204 and 205 (Wikipedia: NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization)
- **Published:** 2024 (Wikipedia: NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization)
- **Purpose:** resist quantum attack (Wikipedia: NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization)
