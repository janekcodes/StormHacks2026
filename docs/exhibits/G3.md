# G3 · Wall of names

## Hook

A museum wall for the hands the plaques never named.

## Caption

The wall of names honours the many contributors to computing whose work went uncredited, from operators and programmers to testers and educators. It is a reminder that every landmark was built by more hands than its plaque can hold.

## Stats

- **Honours:** uncredited contributors (Wikipedia: Women in computing)
- **Example:** the ENIAC Six, recognized late (Wikipedia: Women in computing)
- **Example:** NASA's human computers (Wikipedia: West Area Computers)
