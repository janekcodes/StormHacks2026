# G2 · NASA's human computers

## Hook

The people who did the math that put astronauts in orbit.

## Caption

Before electronic machines, NASA's human computers, many of them Black women, calculated flight and orbital trajectories by hand. Katherine Johnson, Dorothy Vaughan and Mary Jackson became the best known of them.

## Stats

- **Era:** 1940s to 1960s (Wikipedia: West Area Computers)
- **Known members:** Johnson, Vaughan, Jackson (Wikipedia: West Area Computers)
- **Agency:** NACA, then NASA (Wikipedia: West Area Computers)
