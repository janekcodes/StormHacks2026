# E5 · Altair 8800 & Apple II

## Hook

From blinking-front-panel hobby kit to a computer for the home.

## Caption

The Altair 8800, sold in 1975, popularized the microcomputer among hobbyists. The Apple II, shipped in 1977, made computing a home product with colour graphics and a built-in BASIC.

## Stats

- **Altair 8800:** 1975 (Wikipedia: Altair 8800)
- **Apple II:** 1977 (Wikipedia: Apple II)
- **Apple II features:** colour graphics and BASIC (Wikipedia: Apple II)
